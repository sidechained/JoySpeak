How to play

  • Drag Joystick: Start recording movements automatically (LED1 flashes Red). Release to stop.
  • Button 2 (Trigger): Plays back the recorded envelope (modulates the synthesizer!).
  • Button 1 (Rec Mode): Cycles playback behavior: **One-Shot** (Green LED1), **Looping** (Blue LED1), **Overdub** (Cyan LED1).
  • Joystick Button: Cycles through 3 Synthesizer models: **FM**, **Subtractive (Filter Sweep)**, and **Wavefolder**.
