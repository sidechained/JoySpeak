In terms of synthesis model to keep things simple and constrained to begin with let's first take the Mutable Instruments Plaits FM Engine as the base synthesis mode, including amplitude control via low pass gate (LPG). The code for Plaits can be found here: https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack/tree/master/plaits and the FM Engine specifically here: https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack/blob/master/plaits/dsp/engine/fm_engine.cc. The key parameters are: 
- FREQUENCY: fundamental pitch
- HARMONICS: frequency ratio
- TIMBRE: modulation index
- MORPH: feedback, in the form of operator 2 modulating its own phase (past 12 o’clock, rough!) or operator 1’s phase (before 12 o’clock, chaotic!).
- TRIGGER: what causes a sound to play
- LEVEL: the overall volume level of the sound
- DECAY: the decay time/ringing length of the LPG (and simultaneously sets the decay time of the internal modulation envelope

The code should be crafted as to be able to drop in more synthesis models later
